   http://localhost:5000

2. Username
   ✂️ COPY THIS:
   (leave completely empty/blank)

3. Password
   ✂️ COPY THIS:
   (leave completely empty/blank)

4. Auth Token
   ✂️ DO THIS:
   - Open http://localhost:5000 in browser
   - Go to "HRMS Integration" tab
   - Click "Generate Token" button
   - Copy the token that appears
